Career Summary

I'm a detail-oriented, HIPAA-certified Medical Billing Specialist with 7+ years of experience in billing coordination, medical billing, and insurance operations. My background covers the full revenue cycle — charge entry, claims submission, accounts receivable follow-up, denial management, and payer communication — primarily supporting home health and community-based healthcare organizations.

I bring a solid understanding of insurance claims, prior authorization requirements, and healthcare compliance standards, backed by a consistent record of resolving billing discrepancies and appealing denied claims with clear, well-documented submissions. I work equally well operating independently as a contractor and collaborating cross-functionally with billing, clinical, and operations teams.

My goal is straightforward: deliver accurate, compliant, and timely billing services that protect provider revenue while giving patients clarity and confidence in their care experience.
